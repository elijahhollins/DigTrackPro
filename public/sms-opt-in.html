SMS Opt-In & Consent

How DigTrack Pro collects text message consent from every individual user

How Users Opt In

DigTrack Pro is an invite-only application for excavation and utility contractors. A company administrator sends an invite link to a crew member, and that crew member creates their own account.

Each individual user provides their own SMS consent at account creation, inside their own authenticated session. An administrator cannot consent on another user's behalf, and there is no screen anywhere in DigTrack Pro where one user can enter or edit another user's mobile number.

The mobile number field is optional. An account can be created without it, and leaving the consent checkbox unchecked never blocks registration — it simply means that user receives no text messages. Consent is stored per user with a timestamp, the exact version of the consent language shown, the IP address, and the browser user agent.

Users who already had an account before this consent flow existed are shown the same optional prompt once when they next sign in, and can opt in or out at any time from their account settings.

The Opt-In Flow, Step by Step

1

An administrator sends an invite

From Team & Settings, a company administrator generates a single-use invite link and sends it to the crew member. The administrator enters no phone number at any point.

2

The recipient opens the invite link

The link opens the DigTrack Pro registration form, pre-filled with the inviting company. The crew member enters their own name, email address, and password.

3

The recipient enters their number and checks the consent box

Below the password field, the form shows an optional Mobile Number input and a separate, unchecked SMS consent checkbox. The checkbox cannot be checked until a number has been entered, and it is never bundled with terms acceptance.

4

Confirmation

The account is created and the consent decision is stored. The user can see and change their SMS status at any time in Team & Settings, and can turn text alerts off there or by replying STOP to any message.

Consent Language

This is the exact text shown beside the consent checkbox, word for word:

I agree to receive SMS text messages from DigTrack Pro about my locate tickets, including status updates, expiration reminders, and crew assignment alerts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Sample Messages

Expiration reminder DigTrack Pro: Locate ticket 2604123 for 1400 W Main St expires 08/14. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
Crew assignment alert DigTrack Pro: You've been assigned to Job #4471 — 220 Oak Ave. Locate marks are valid through 08/20.
Status update DigTrack Pro: Ticket 2604877 is now marked clear by all utilities. Msg & data rates may apply.

Message frequency varies with each user's ticket activity. Message and data rates may apply. Users can reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for support information.

Opting Out

  • Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT to any message
  • Or turn off Text Alerts in Team & Settings inside the app
  • Either method stops all SMS immediately; opting out never affects access to DigTrack Pro
  • Reply HELP to any message, or email hello@digtrack.app, for support
